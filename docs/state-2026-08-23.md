## 4. What the overnight shift settled

**Depth 6 costs ~36 h and ~74 GB at 8 threads** (`results/depth6-cost-settled.md`).
The asserted 20–60 h / 50–100 GB holds, unlike depth 5's assertion which was an
order of magnitude out. RAM forces 8 threads (16 threads projects to ~148 GB and
does not fit dalby), so the wall is 1.5–2 days. The assertion's *method* was
wrong even so: the per-excess factor grows from 6.1× to 10.4× across K = 8..14,
so pricing depth 7 the same way would be badly wrong.

**The maximum-hole formula survived its first real test**
(`results/maxhole-closed-form.md`). `n − ⌈2√n⌉ + 1` predicted 5 at n = 11 and
the exhaustive sweep returned 5. The run's own eleventh animal count,
`a(11) = 39,299,408`, matches A006770 — an external check the file could not
give itself.

**The frontier census measured H = 14, 15 and 16** at 53,763, 136,145 and
346,539 (`results/nkey-census.md`), replacing extrapolation at three heights and
showing where the method stops: cost ratios 6.7 then 5.3 per height, so H = 18
is three days and H = 21 is out of reach without sharing partial fills between
source states.

**A1.3 is closed and its cause moved** (`results/dmirror-spine-split.md`). The
diagonal-mirror family fails the grand form; splitting it by spine shows the
main-diagonal family is linear at four consecutive cumulants and the
anti-diagonal family fails at the second. So the obstruction is one specific
family, not the fact that two were summed. Along the way the main family's
levels turned out to have degree `⌊k/2⌋` with no parity dependence, measured at
seven consecutive levels and used successfully as a prediction three times.

**L10 has had its priority pass and it found a near neighbour**
(`docs/priority-pass-L10-2026-08-23.md`): correcting finite-size data by a known
error term is standard practice in directed-percolation series work. Cited; the
paper now claims only the combination that survives.

**Two hazards found by reading code, neither caught by a gate**: the census's
retirement rule could file one component as two, and the spine enumerator's
4-bit label field would alias past 15 components. Both fixed; the census fix
reproduces H = 14 and H = 15 exactly.

**The square-lattice record is n = 70, not 56** — known in one file since
2026-08-1x and unswept until now. Six live sites corrected,
`results/literature-record-56-corrected.md` is the one place, no conclusion
moves.

## 5. Open, priced, and unlaunched

Each of these is a run or a build that nobody has authorised.

**The five terms.** With depth 6 measured, reaching n ≤ 45 means the H = 20
sweep at Nmax 45 (~277 GB disk, fits dalby's 563 GB free) plus a `families 21 5`
run (~36–44 h, ~74–85 GB, 8 threads). The alternative route through H = 21 needs
~580 GB and does not fit. **Blocked behind review row B13**, which requires the
depth-5 gate to pass at k ≤ 19 before the depth-5 defect is used at k = 21 —
that gate is correctly RED until the depth-5 table exists, and depth 6 is one
rung further out than that.

**The frontier census past H = 17.** Reaching H = 18..21 needs partial fills
shared between source states, the way the production engine's carry does it,
rather than rebuilt per source. A day's build, gated by the same ladder that
gates the current one. What it buys: the H = 20 and H = 21 decisions get a
measured frontier instead of a six-step extrapolation.

**The rank test at H = 13.** The strip automaton's characteristic-2 rank
collapses to A034299's values at H ≤ 12; H = 13 predicts 3,643. ~2 h on top of a
build already done. It needs a second source before it counts as more than
suggestive. What it buys: a fifth confirmation of the identification that is the
only handle anyone has on the largest open technical question in the reach
mission — an explicit basis for that collapse.

**Certified brackets for the short-rook lattice.** λ ≈ 8.97 there is a
calibrated estimate, not a bound. Making the certificate program
lattice-parametric and running it per lattice gives proved two-sided brackets,
which do not appear to exist for more than one or two lattices anywhere. A build
plus per-lattice runs; king H = 18 was tens of minutes and tens of GB and the
short-rook strip is probably worse. It shortens no sentence in the polyplet
papers and is the material of a separate short paper.

**c₆ on the main-diagonal spine family.** Needs S = 20 and S = 21 of the spine
enumerator, about a day at the measured 3.5× per S. Whether four linear
cumulants is already enough to believe that family has a grand form is a
judgement rather than a measurement.
