  on the final row sum alone, and at n = 48 that sum is within about 10% of
  2^128; intermediate multi-component counts exceed the row sum. Silent wrap
  is prevented only by `assert(slot >= prev)` in `core/run.h:116,132,138`,
  live because `NSFLAGS` has no `-DNDEBUG`, while `core/counter.h` still says
  overflow is silent. Make the checks unconditional and measure the largest

  depth 1. It is not: `pp[e][k]` enters `D_{e+1}(k)` with coefficient 1. The
  mutation sample was `e <= 2`, which feeds depths the gate does not check.
  Good direction, but `pp[3][21]` is one of M1's three integers, with the
  largest lever.

  (all seven banked tables are complete today).

  assembler to the wired value with no re-pin; assert `max(read_pk()) == 19`.
  Also `kfact int64` cannot hold 21!.
