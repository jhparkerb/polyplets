Non-D-finiteness of anisotropic generating functions (the lineage our theorem sits in):
  bousquet-melou_rechnitzer_2002_lattice_animals_heaps_of_dimers.pdf
                                           Bousquet-Melou & Rechnitzer, Lattice animals and heaps of
                                           dimers, Discrete Math. 258 (2002) 235-274. **Lemma 9 is the
                                           source criterion**: if S(q,u) is D-finite in u with coefficients
                                           rational in q, the union of the coefficients' pole sets has
                                           finitely many limit points. Both Haruspicy papers cite this,
                                           not Haruspicy 1, for the D-finiteness test. Free from
                                           labri.fr/perso/bousquet (PostScript; converted here).
  rechnitzer_2006_haruspicy2_sap_not_dfinite.pdf
                                           Rechnitzer, Haruspicy 2: the anisotropic GF of self-avoiding
                                           polygons is not D-finite, JCTA 113 (2006) 520-546
                                           (arXiv math/0406450). Criterion restated as Theorem 15.
  rechnitzer_2006_haruspicy3_bond_animals_not_dfinite.pdf
                                           Rechnitzer, Haruspicy 3: the directed bond-animal GF is not
                                           D-finite, JCTA (2006) (arXiv math/0408054). Same criterion as
                                           Theorem 5.
  rechnitzer_2003_haruspicy1_anisotropic_gf.pdf
                                           Rechnitzer, Haruspicy and anisotropic generating functions,
                                           Adv. in Appl. Math. 30 (2003) 228-257. Obtained 2026-08-01
                                           (jasonp, ScienceDirect). Supplies the combinatorial
                                           section/density machinery -- for a wide range of bond-animal
                                           families the coefficient of y^n is rational, numerator degree
                                           <= denominator degree, denominator a product of cyclotomic
                                           polynomials, with a tight multiplicative upper bound. NOT the
                                           source of the D-finiteness test (that is BM-R 2002, above).
                                           Section 2 explains the name: haruspicy is divination by
                                           examining the organs of animals, and the technique examines
                                           bond configurations inside lattice animals.
  klazar_2003_non_p_recursiveness_matchings.pdf
                                           Klazar, Non-P-recursiveness of numbers of matchings (linear
                                           chord diagrams) with many crossings, Adv. in Appl. Math. 30
                                           (2003) 126-136. Same issue; cited by Haruspicy 2 as [13].
                                           Adjacent prior art on proving sequences non-P-recursive.
                                           Obstruction is ANALYTIC (divergence); introduces D_A-finite.
  bell_gerhold_klazar_luca_2008_nonholonomicity_elementary_functions.pdf
                                           Bell, Gerhold, Klazar & Luca, Non-holonomicity of sequences
                                           defined via elementary functions, Ann. Comb. (2008),
                                           arXiv math/0605142. Analytic method (counting zeros); class is
                                           elementary functions evaluated at integers. Swept 2026-08-01
                                           as part of closing the Northcott novelty item; no collision.

Lattice-animal literature obtained 2026-08-01 (jasonp, ScienceDirect):
  barequet_benshachar_osegueda_2021_concatenation_arguments.pdf
                                           Barequet, Ben-Shachar & Osegueda, Concatenation arguments and
                                           their applications to polyominoes and polycubes, Comput. Geom.
                                           98 (2021) 101790. Fekete on quasi sub-/super-multiplicative
                                           sequences => growth-constant bounds. NOT a log-convexity tool
                                           (C2 unmoved); the sub-multiplicative UPPER-bound direction is
                                           the part that could bear on our 9.3153. See MISSING.md.
  aleksandrowicz_barequet_2009_polycubes_dimensionality_curse.pdf
                                           Aleksandrowicz & Barequet, Counting polycubes without the
                                           dimensionality curse, Discrete Math. 309 (2009) 4576-4583.
                                           Face-adjacency hypercubic (2d neighbours); generalizes by
                                           DIMENSION, not adjacency -- confirmed does not touch king.
  mansour_rastegar_2021_animals_triangular_lattice.pdf
                                           Mansour & Rastegar, Enumeration of various animals on the
                                           triangular lattice, European J. Combin. 94 (2021) 103294
                                           (arXiv 2011.05318). Baryiamonds, column-convex and convex
                                           polyiamonds by perimeter. Bears on universality (polyiamonds).
  liu_wang_2007_log_convexity_combinatorial_sequences.pdf
                                           Liu & Wang, On the log-convexity of combinatorial sequences,
                                           Adv. Appl. Math. 39 (2007) 453-476 (arXiv math/0602672).
                                           Closure operations + three-term recurrences; a(n) has no known
                                           recurrence, so probably cannot engage C2. Relevant to C2.
  gouyou-beauchamps_viennot_1988_directed_animal_path_equivalence.pdf
                                           Gouyou-Beauchamps & Viennot, Adv. Appl. Math. 9 (1988)
                                           334-357. Directed animals <-> 1D paths; the lineage behind the
                                           Bacher comparison bound and BM-R's heaps.
  bacher_2015_directed_multidirected_king_lattice.pdf
                                           Bacher, Directed and multi-directed animals in the king's
                                           lattice, arXiv:1301.1365v3 (2015). Obtained 2026-08-05. THE
                                           source for both king-lattice bounds: Theorem 4 (directed GF =
                                           A047781, growth 3+2sqrt2), Definition 2 (multi-directed),
                                           Theorem 8 (Nordic decomposition, M = D/(1-B)), Lemma 11
                                           (rho_B, root of 1-5x-7x^2+x^3 -- the 6.118 DECOY, not the
                                           growth constant), Theorem 10 / Corollary 12 (B(rho_M)=1,
                                           mu = 6.4752). Implemented in results/multi-directed.md.
  bousquet-melou_1993_q_enumeration_polyominos_convexes.pdf
                                           Bousquet-Melou, q-Enumeration de polyominos convexes, JCTA 64
                                           (1993) 265-288. For the parked convex-polyplets side quest.

Growth-rate bounding methods (obtained 2026-08-01, for the lambda <= 9.3153 push):
  chan_rechnitzer_2018_upper_bounds_growth_rates_corner_transfer_matrices.pdf
                                           Chan & Rechnitzer, Linear Algebra Appl. 555 (2018) 139-156.
                                           Calkin-Wilf eigenvalue upper bound + Collatz-Wielandt, with the

                                           extending our mu_H ladder past H=17. See
                                           results/strip-growth-lambda-bounds.md.
