%N Axxxxx-asymmetric Number of asymmetric free polyplets with n cells (king-move animals, A030222, with trivial symmetry group).
%C Axxxxx-asymmetric A polyplet (polyking) is a set of unit cells joined through shared edges or corners. The square symmetry group D4 partitions the free polyplets A030222 into 8 classes by exact symmetry group; this is one of them. The five reflection-containing classes (axial, diagonal, two-axis, two-diagonal, full) sum to A030234 (bilaterally symmetric); the other three (asymmetric, 180-degree, 90-degree) sum to A030235. Polyplet analogue of A006749 (polyominoes). - _Jason H Parker_, Jul 05 2026
