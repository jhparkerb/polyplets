%N Axxxxxn Minimum number of cells in a polyplet (king-move animal, A006770) that encloses k holes, where a hole is a bounded 4-connected component of the complement; a(k) = min{ n : some fixed n-cell polyplet has at least k holes }.
%C Axxxxxn a(k) = min{ n : maxh(n) >= k }, the inverse of maxh(n) = n + 1 - A027709(n)/2, the maximum number of holes of an n-cell polyplet (A027709 = minimum perimeter of an n-cell polyomino). The hole-densest shape is the even sublattice {x+y even} of a minimum-perimeter polyomino: those cells are king-connected and, by Pick's theorem, enclose n + 1 - A027709(n)/2 single-cell holes.
%C Axxxxxn maxh(n) increases by 0 or 1 at each step, so every hole-count k is attained and a(k) is strictly increasing; a(k) ~ k (each extra cell adds about one hole), in contrast to the single large diamond, whose hole AREA is ~n^2/8. - _Jason H Parker_, 2026
%e Axxxxxn a(1)=4: four cells at the edge-midpoints of a unit square (a diamond ring) enclose the center cell as one hole.
%e Axxxxxn a(4)=9: the nine cells with x+y even inside |x|+|y|<=2 (a 3 X 3 block in the rotated lattice) enclose 4 single-cell holes.
%Y Axxxxxn Cf. A006770 (fixed polyplets), A027709 (minimum polyomino perimeter); inverse of the max-hole-count sequence n+1-A027709(n)/2.
