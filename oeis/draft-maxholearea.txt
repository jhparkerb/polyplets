%N Axxxxxc Maximum hole area of an n-cell polyplet: the largest number of enclosed empty cells over all fixed n-cell king-move animals (A006770), where a hole is a bounded 4-connected component of the complement.
%C Axxxxxc A polyplet (polyking) is a set of unit cells connected through edges or corners. With the 4-connected-background convention a diagonal staircase of cells seals a region, so the area-maximizing shape is an L1 diamond: a king-wall of 4r cells on |x|+|y|=r encloses the radius-(r-1) diamond, of area 2r^2-2r+1.
%F Axxxxxc a(n) = ceil(floor((n-2)^2/4)/2) for n >= 4 (and a(1)=a(2)=a(3)=0). By residue mod 4: a(4r)=2r^2-2r+1 (A001844), a(4r+1)=2r^2-r, a(4r+2)=2r^2, a(4r+3)=2r^2+r. Equivalently a(n) = ceil(A002620(n-2)/2).
%C Axxxxxc The closed form is EXACT for every n (a theorem), not merely a bound. Proof sketch: rotate to (u,v) = (x+y, x-y); every king step changes (u,v) by total L1-length |du|+|dv| = 2*max(|dx|,|dy|) = 2, so any closed king-wall of length L satisfies L >= (u-extent) + (v-extent). Enclosing a hole forces the wall one cell beyond it on each side, so wall >= (a) + (c) + 4 where a,c are the hole's u- and v-extents; with n cells this gives a + c <= n - 4. The hole lies on the even sublattice of its (u,v) bounding box, at most ceil((a+1)(c+1)/2) cells, maximized (AM-GM) by the square = the L1 diamond; the balanced rectangular L1-diamond meets the bound. The diamond is the unique maximizer (up to translation). This is the exact discrete counterpart of the L1 (Busemann) isoperimetric inequality, whose continuous form gives only a(n) ~ n^2/8.
%C Axxxxxc a(1..16) were independently obtained by exhaustive enumeration of all fixed polyplets (Redelmeier with hole-area tracking) and agree with the formula. Edge-connected polyominoes, which cannot seal with diagonal walls, reach only ~n^2/16.
%e Axxxxxc a(4)=1: four cells at the N, E, S, W of an empty center form a diamond ring (each consecutive pair king-adjacent) that seals the center cell as a hole.
%e Axxxxxc a(8)=5: the diamond wall |x|+|y|=2 (8 cells) encloses the 5 cells with |x|+|y|<=1.
