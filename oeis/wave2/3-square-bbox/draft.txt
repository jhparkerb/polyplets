%N Axxxxxc Number of fixed n-cell polyplets (A006770) whose minimal bounding box is a square (width = height).
%C Axxxxxc A polyplet (or polyking) is a set of unit cells connected through edges or corners (king moves); A006770 counts the fixed (translations-only) ones. Here we count those whose minimal axis-aligned bounding box has equal width and height.
%C Axxxxxc Summing this subclass over all bounding-box shapes recovers A006770: sum over (w,h) of the by-bounding-box counts equals A006770(n). The diagonal w = h is this sequence.
%C Axxxxxc Computed by direct Redelmeier enumeration of fixed polyplets, binning each by its bounding-box (width, height); the full (w,h) marginal was checked to equal A006770(n) term-by-term through n = 16. - _Jason H Parker_, 2026
