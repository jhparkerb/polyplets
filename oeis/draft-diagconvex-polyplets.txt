%N Axxxxxi Number of fixed diagonally-convex polyplets with n cells: king-move animals (A006770) each of whose diagonals (cells with x+y constant) and antidiagonals (cells with x-y constant) is a contiguous run.
%C Axxxxxi A polyplet (polyking) is a set of unit cells connected through edges or corners. In the rotated coordinates (u,v) = (x+y, x-y) the king animal must be convex along both u and v: each diagonal x+y=const and each antidiagonal x-y=const meets the figure in a single gap-free run. This is the diagonal analogue of HV-convexity (Axxxxxh); for king animals it is the less restrictive of the two, since cells extend naturally along diagonals.
%C Axxxxxi a(3)=20=A006770(3): every 3-cell polyplet is diagonally convex. "Fixed" = up to translation only. Computed by exhaustive enumeration of fixed polyplets (canonical growth), per-size totals verified against A006770 through n=9 (experiments/convex_polyplets.py). - _Jason H Parker_, 2026
