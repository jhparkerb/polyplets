%N A030222 Number of n-polyplets (polyominoes connected at edges or corners); may contain holes.
%C A030222 See A056840 for illustrations, valid also for this sequence up to n=4, but slightly misleading for polyplets with holes. See the colored areas in the illustration of A056840(5)=99 which correspond to identical 5-polyplets. (The 2+2+4-3 = 5 additional figures counted there correspond to the 4-square configuration with a hole inside ({2,4,6,8} on a numeric keyboard), with one additional square added in three inequivalent places: "inside" one angle (touching two sides), attached to one side, and attached to a corner. These do only count for 3 here, but for 8 in A056840.) It can be seen that A056840 counts a sort of "spanning trees" instead, i.e., simply connected graphs that connect all of the vertices (using only "King's moves", and maybe other additional constraints). - _M. F. Hasler_, Sep 29 2014
%C A030222 a(18) and a(19) computed via Burnside's lemma over the dihedral group D4 from the fixed-polyplet counts A006770 and the rotation/reflection fixed-point counts; a(19) rests on the independently confirmed A006770(19) = 151609203011580. - _Jason H Parker_, Jul 05 2026
%C A030222 a(20)-a(32) (in the b-file) derived by the same Burnside identity from the extended A006770 (see that entry's Comments for the confidence discussion of its single-algorithm terms) and symmetric fixed-point counts computed by symmetric transfer-matrix engines, validated against a symmetry-classified direct enumeration for n <= 24; the /8 integrality holds at every n. Reach is capped at n=32 by the diagonal-mirror count. - _Jason H Parker_, Jul 05 2026
%C A030222 Conjecturally a(33) = 9328935247555296909698722: the diagonal-mirror fixed-point count D(33) it requires was assembled from exact strip computations plus empirically pinned but unproven quasi-polynomial formulas for the sparsest strips (validated against every directly computed overlap cell, but conjecture-assisted nonetheless); kept as a comment rather than b-file data. Independent confirmation welcome. - _Jason H Parker_, Jul 06 2026
