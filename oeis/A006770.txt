%N A006770 Number of fixed n-celled polyominoes which need only touch at corners.
%C A006770 Also known as fixed polyplets. - _David Bevan_, Jul 28 2009
%C A006770 a(19)-a(22) are confirmed by two independent algorithms sharing no counting logic: direct Redelmeier enumeration (run as a three-machine, three-architecture fleet; ~120 hours of wall time per machine for n = 22) and a column transfer matrix, agreeing digit-for-digit on every n <= 22, with the smaller terms re-derived on every run as a by-product. - _Jason H Parker_, Jul 16 2026
%C A006770 a(23)-a(40) (in the b-file) were computed by the same column/kink-carry transfer-matrix engine: each run recomputes the whole height triangle T(n,H) -- low heights by direct sweep, top heights by validated closed-form height-diagonals -- byte-matching all prior terms, with a full cross-ISA (ARM vs x86) recount of the entire n <= 34 triangle at a(34), and with every closed-form diagonal certified by later real sweeps fitted only on earlier terms: the held-out formulas P_15, P_16 and P_17 predicted the real-swept cells T(33,18), T(35,19) and T(37,20) exactly, and the a(40) run's first-ever real height-21 sweep reproduced the closed-form column-21 values on every row n = 21..39 at once -- P_18's first holdout, T(39,21), among them. These are single-algorithm results; two-algorithm independent confirmation (the standard met through a(22)) is infeasible much beyond n ~ 22, since Redelmeier generation is O(a(n)). - _Jason H Parker_, Jul 16 2026
