%N A006770 Number of fixed n-celled polyominoes which need only touch at corners.
%C A006770 Also known as fixed polyplets. - _David Bevan_, Jul 28 2009
%C A006770 a(19)-a(22) are confirmed by two independent algorithms sharing no counting logic: direct Redelmeier enumeration (run as a three-machine, three-architecture fleet; ~120 hours of wall time per machine for n = 22) and a column transfer matrix, agreeing digit-for-digit on every n <= 22, with the smaller terms re-derived on every run as a by-product. - _Jason H Parker_, ~~~~
%C A006770 a(23)-a(40) (in the b-file) were computed by a column transfer matrix over the bounding-box height: each run recomputes the whole triangle T(n,H) of fixed polyplets of size n and height exactly H, sweeping the short heights directly and evaluating proved closed forms for the tall ones, and byte-matching every earlier term. Above the line H > n/2 the triangle satisfies T(n,H) = P_{n-H}(n)*3^(3H-2n-1) with P_k an integer-valued polynomial of degree k; the shape, its exact range of validity and the fact that two computed entries determine each P_k are proved, and formalized in Lean 4 against standard axioms. Each closed form was fitted only on earlier terms and then held out against a later real sweep: P_15, P_16, P_17 and P_18 predicted T(33,18), T(35,19), T(37,20) and T(39,21) exactly. The n <= 34 triangle was independently recounted on a second architecture. - _Jason H Parker_, ~~~~
%C A006770 A second enumeration, sharing no counting logic with the first, covers the terms past the reach of Redelmeier enumeration: a transfer matrix that counts by coloring cells and never decides connectivity, run to height 19. For 23 <= n <= 39 every entry of the triangle is either recounted by that program or given by a closed form whose two constants are fixed from that program's own entries, so no term in that range depends on the first program. At n = 40 the coloring program reaches 19 of the 40 heights and agrees on all of them. Three entries of the whole table have one enumeration behind them and no closed form: T(39,20), T(40,20) and T(40,21). - _Jason H Parker_, ~~~~
