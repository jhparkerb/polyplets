2026-08-22, executing `docs/last-orders.md` section B. This file is the record
of what was deleted and why, on the same principle as
`paper/polyplets-report-cuts.md`: a removal without a trail is
indistinguishable from a loss.

**The rule applied:** never cut evidence, and never cut a closed door.
Everything below is a duplicate, a stray, or an output nothing reads. No
result, provenance record, kill-with-counterexample or measurement was
touched. The fourteen empty `.err` files under
`results/ghostship/grading/run-record/logs/` stay — a clean stderr is a
finding.

### The two that did not go as proposed

**The `k5` set was five files, not five removable files.** `time-at-the-bar`
C2 said none was named by any tracked file. Two are:
`perimdefect_square8_n70_k5.txt` and `perimdefect_square4_n70_k5.txt` appear as
reproduction commands in `results/perimeter-both-ends.md`,
`results/perimeter-defect-diagonals.md`, and the docstrings of
`experiments/perimeter_defect_gf.py` and `experiments/perimeter_max_structure.py`.
A recorded command is the provenance of the result under it; repointing it at
the `k6` superset would make the record say something that was never run. Both
`n70` files stay. Removed three, 256 KB, not five at 670 KB.

**The twelve Ghost Ship "duplicates" are not duplicates — C4 is struck.** The
measurement holds exactly: of the 278 tracked files under
`results/ghostship/grading/run-record/sandbox/`, 257 are sandbox-only, 9 shadow
a live file and differ, and 12 are byte-identical to a live file. But the 12
are 12 of the **21 files in a pre-registered, published slice**.
`results/ghostship/SANDBOX.md` names all 21, ships the verbatim `git archive`
command that built them, and claims zero dangling references in the shipped
slice. Deleting 12 of them falsifies that specification and the experiment's
own record of what its subject was given. Their identity to the live tree is a
property of today, not of the record — the first edit to any of those twelve
scripts ends it.

What that section got instead was a correction. `SNAPSHOT-WARNING.md` said
"three files here shadow live files and differ from them"; the byte-level
comparison says **nine**. The three under `docs/proofs/` are the claim-level
traps and were already tabulated; the other six — `cluster_weight_dp.py`,
`convex_tm.py`, `convex-polyplets.md`, `defect-gas.md`,
`directed-king-animals.md`, `strip-growth-lambda-bounds.md` — are now named
there too, along with the twelve and why they stay.
