## b2 SETTLED
M_asym(n) = 0,0,0,0,1,1,2,3,5,7,9,11 (n=1..12). The earlier conjecture M_asym(n)=M(n-1)
held only n<=9 (a coincidence) and is conclusively REFUTED: at n=10,11,12 M_asym = 7,9,11
while M(n-1) = 6,8,10. The real M_asym hugs M(n) at an IRREGULAR gap of 1-2
(M-M_asym = 1,0,1,1,2,1,1,1,2 for n=4..12) -- no clean closed form. Durable facts: the
maxhole optimum is always symmetric (M_sym=M), and asymmetry costs a small irregular premium.

## multihole M_k (C2)
The per-k maxima are NON-monotone in k and have no evident closed form (e.g. n=12:
M_1..M_6 = 13,9,8,6,5,6 -- M_6 > M_5). Extends the earlier n<=9 brute force to n=12.

## build note
build/g2 must be compiled with clang directly (the Makefile's gcc-only -Wno-error=restrict
breaks clang); clang doesn't trip the -Wrestrict false positive, so it's a no-op loss.
