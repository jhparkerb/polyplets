## The frame the rows came from

The round's three floors (state, information, term-source) bound three
CLASSES: frontier vocabularies, straight-cut methods, and fitted fixed-H
recurrences. The surviving package (L3-5 proof + L6-1 ladder + INV-8 parity)
attacks the rule and the count. What the floors do NOT bound, and where the
entry ticket has already twice been found blind (binary provenance,
archived-state replay), is everything that is *about the banked run and its
artifacts rather than about counting*: telemetry, coverage, analytic
structure, accounting scope, and exactness itself. Most of the rows live
there deliberately.

## The strongest rows, in the order I'd defend them

### GEN-1 — transpose the sweep at H=21 (the claim-correction that changes phase 2)

The synthesis says "H=21 does not fit ayr under any bookkeeping" (215.8 GiB,
ADV-I4's exact census). That is a statement about the sweep orientation, not
the cell. A king-connected animal cannot cross an empty row (Δy ≤ 1 per
step), so every bounding-box row is occupied and H = 21 forces W ≤ 40−21+1
= 20. Sweep along the height and the cut is the *width*: the census is a
function of strip height only, so the transposed state count is ADV-I4's
H=20 number — 703,470,478 windows, 68.1 GiB at the model constant. And the
transpose is a strict simplification, not a trade: height-exactness, which
the normal orientation buys with a second-difference layer, becomes
sweep-direction spanning (first/last sweep column nonempty — at most 2-3
fixed-length runs by inclusion-exclusion), while the transverse direction
needs no exactness at all because T(40,21) sums over widths.

L6-2 used exactly this orientation trick for the spin basis; nobody applied
it to the partition-basis ladder. The reason to rank it first: it collapses
the phase-2 shape from "base + option + off-fleet stretch" to "the whole
band, gated on one measurable constant" — after LG-2's container pessimism,
H=20 and H=21-transposed share the same flat-double-buffer design gate
(GEN-C5). Kill is seconds: a transposed-box variant of the census script,
plus the desk argument above surviving hostile read. The one honest risk is
kink-window slot bookkeeping differing between orientations.

### GEN-2 — telemetry vs theorem on the banked run (audit the computation that actually ran)

New information crossed with new information. PROV established that the
production run.log survives on dalby, build-stamped; I verified in-tree
(`orchestrator/telemetry.go:125-129`) that every `event=column` line carries
`frontier_in`/`frontier_out` — per-column frontier record counts, for every
column of every in-band height. Separately, the round built exact
state-census machinery (masks-over-chunks: L4-11, ADV-I4). Put together: the
per-column reachable-state counts of the executed sweep are *predictable by
theorem* (under width/budget truncation, which the same path-DP method
handles), and the prediction can be compared against the surviving log,
column by column, with zero recount.

This is the only instrument anyone has proposed that audits the *executed
computation's internal structure* — it can catch lost shards, truncated
frontiers, and resume faults, which is precisely the residual class L3-5
names as unreachable ("a theorem about the schema does not re-run it") and
which L6-1 reaches only through final-number agreement. Like the two
provenance findings, it is unstateable in the entry ticket's vocabulary,
which is presumably why three rounds never filed it. The honest risk, stated
in the row: "records" may fold in n-range sharding, in which case only
shape/monotonicity checks survive. Step 1 is a semantics read
(`docs/formats.md`, `sweep.go:786`), not a computation.

### GEN-3 — configuration coverage: how thin is the external pinning, as a number

The lazy referee response to this whole round is "the engines agree with
no-frontier oracles at every n ≤ 22; a rule error would have shown there."
The rebuttal exists but is qualitative. Make it a measurement: for each
reachable frontier state at H = 15..21, compute the minimal total cell
budget to reach *and complete* it. States above 22 were never exercised in
any externally-locked cell — the hypothesised misconception, if it exists,
lives exactly in that never-witnessed set, and at H = 15 with only 7 spare
cells over the height minimum, that set is plausibly almost everything.
Either outcome is content: a large never-witnessed share is the
quantitative justification for L3-5+L6-1 (and the sharpened epitaph); a
small one is genuine good news nobody currently possesses. Different in kind
because it treats the rule as software under test with coverage
measurement — no other row does.

### GEN-5 — the kink-schema equivalence lemma (shrink the proof's residual)

L3-5 proves the *column* schema. The banked band was swept by the *kink*
kernel — cell-at-a-time, NW carry. So the proof route's (b)–(c) residual
today contains a whole clause that nobody has priced: "the kink schema is
the column schema." That is an abstract statement — one lemma, that the
cell-at-a-time step with the carried NW label, composed over a column,
equals the column step — and it is provable at the definition level without
touching packed signatures. If it lands, the residual after L3-5 contracts
to pure C++ transcription; if the statement cannot even be written without
implementation detail, it collapses into (c) and dies in one honest
session. This is the only row that makes the round's flagship proof cover
the binary that actually produced the exposure.

### GEN-11 — vary exactness (the axis no lane touched)

Every lane varied the rule or the reach; none varied *exactness*. A
Knuth/SIS unbiased estimator over the Redelmeier growth tree of band
animals is rooted growth — no frontier, no labels, no completion predicate;
both ticket levels clear for what it is — and unbiasedness is a proof, not
a hope. It would put a few-percent confidence interval on T(40,H) by a
no-frontier rule at n = 40, today, at laptop-day cost, with no phase-2
build. L6's F1 filter killed Monte Carlo *as a route to exact counts*;
as a gross-fault check on 50.84% of a(40) it was never evaluated. Prior
0.35, because tree-estimator variance on animal trees may be unusable at
n = 40 — and the kill is cheap and quantitative (measure relative sigma
against banked values at n = 12..22; if it grows exponentially past
n ≈ 25, file the curve and close). Even the kill is a deliverable: nobody
knows the variance curve of the one estimator class that reaches the band.

### GEN-6 — claim what the ladder already buys (hundreds of cells, not seven)

The L6-1 payload is area-graded: one pass at height H produces residues of
T(n,H) for every n ≤ 40. The exposure map is row-40-centric, but rows
~23..39 in the band are equally single-sweep, and the related-sequences
chain consumes them. Zero marginal cost; purely a claiming obligation. LG-1
then demonstrated the point in data before this file was finished: the
"H ≤ 16 calibration" run turned out to be worth 21.64% of a(40) — 640
cells — precisely because it carried all n. The row stands as the phase-2
wording obligation for the remaining heights.

### GEN-7 + GEN-8 — the two remaining afternoon-shaped unclaimed items

GEN-7: A335606 carries a published order-14 recurrence; extending it to
n = 40 yields the only external per-height number obtainable *at the
mission's own n* on any lattice, and the rook-schema DP computes the
matching value in seconds. Depth at 40, complementing ADV3-JOB-1's breadth
at 14. GEN-8: the strip_tm transitive-input audit is the harness's second
NOT ESTABLISHED item and has now sat unclaimed for a round — the same shape
the provenance audit had before PROV claimed it and it paid. Neither is
glamorous; both are exactly the "high value, low cost, invisible because
nobody's lane owned it" class the dispatch asked me to hunt.

### GEN-4, GEN-9, GEN-10 — filed with their kills, because the kills are the content

GEN-4 (certified mu_H brackets + ratio smoothness + supermultiplicativity
on band columns) is the one proved-*inequality* instrument nobody filed;
its kill — quantify the smallest detectable corruption before trusting a
pass — is written into the row so it cannot be oversold. GEN-9 closes, at
filing, a door a future round would otherwise open: reader-checkable ladder
certificates die on the same compilation floor as counters, so "why is your
certificate smaller than the cut information?" joins L6-8's triage test.
GEN-10 records that the free Klein-parity bit on band-adjacent odd-n cells
is already banked (135/135 holdout to n = 40), so nobody rediscovers it.

## The single most promising unexplored direction

**The surviving run record as a verification substrate.** The round's two
cheapest real findings (binary provenance, the completed-but-unexamined
dalby B1 run) and the two strongest new rows here (GEN-2 telemetry-vs-
theorem, GEN-1's orientation correction) share one shape: the project now
owns exact structural theory (censuses, Motzkin identities, provenance
banners) that the *already-executed* computations can be tested against,
and the artifacts to test — 3.8 MB of build-stamped per-column telemetry,
checkpoints, possibly in-band shards — are sitting on one machine,
unbanked, one `rm -rf` from gone. Everything else in the round proposes
new computation; this direction interrogates computation that already
happened, at reading cost. PROV-2 (bank the evidence) is therefore not
housekeeping — it is preserving the substrate the next wave of checks
runs on.
